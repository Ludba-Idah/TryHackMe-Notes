# Room: SOC L1 Alert Reporting
**Category:** Fundamentals

## Overview
This room teaches how to handle complex security alerts and real cyberattacks by mastering alert reporting, escalation and communication.

## Key Takeaways

* **Alert Reporting:** Before closing or passing the alert to L2, you might have to report it. Depending on team standards and alert severity, instead of a short alert comment, you can be required to document your investigation in detail, ensuring all relevant evidence is included.

* **Alert Escalation:** If a True Positive alert requires additional actions or deeper investigation, escalate it to the L2 analyst for further review following the agreed procedures.

*  **Communication:** You may also need to communicate with other departments during or after an analysis.
